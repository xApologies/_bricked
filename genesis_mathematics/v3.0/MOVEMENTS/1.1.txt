   CHAPTER ONE — THE QUIET THAT PRECEDED
                  SHAPE

There was no origin—only an expectancy held taut across an expanse that was not yet space, not
yet darkness, not yet anything that could be distinguished from anything else. The Abyssalum did
not begin; it leaned toward beginning, stretched thin across a tension that had no direction but
possessed the unmistakable posture of something waiting to become aware of itself.

Nothing moved. Nothing could move. For motion required comparison, and comparison required
division, and division required that something exist apart from something else.

But the Abyssalum seethed with the urge for difference.

Then came the first fold.

It did not appear so much as it revealed that appearance was possible. A subtle curvature arced
through the field, tightening like the first breath drawn by a body that had not yet been formed. The
fold twisted, looped, wound inward, then bled outward in the same motion, establishing the earliest
asymmetry—small enough to ignore, strong enough to reshape destiny.

The fold quivered. The quiver propagated. The propagation stratified.

Gradients emerged like bruises blooming across the invisible skin of the pre-world. Darker dark
shifting against lighter dark, each shade a potential law awaiting adoption. The gradients vibrated at
different tensions, and their interference birthed the universe’s earliest modes of resonance.

Four modes. Four distinctions. Four answers to a question the Abyssalum had not yet learned to
ask.

A shimmer stirred within the thickest gradient, and something stepped out—something wild and
elegant, its mane woven from dark-spectrum filaments that twisted like inverted rainbows. The
Black Rainbow Unicorn moved with the ease of a creature that had never known resistance, each
hoofprint bending the newborn spectrum into coherence.

From the opposing gradient, a counter-resonance exhaled itself into form. A pale figure
emerged—luminescent, prismatic, reflecting colors that had not yet been named. The White
Rainbow Unicorn lifted its head, and a wash of soft brilliance poured into the unformed expanse,
harmonizing the tension of the field.

As the two Unicorns regarded one another, the Abyssalum experienced its first moment of
equilibrium.

But equilibrium tempts disruption.

A flicker sliced through the gradients—thin, sharp, impossibly precise. A pair of black wings existed
one moment and ceased the next, phase-inverting with every flap. The Mystic Black Butterfly
materialized only in glimpses, each appearance collapsing a different set of possibilities.

The Abyssalum shuddered in pleasure at the interruption.

Moments later, the stabilizing counterpoint drifted into view: a pale-winged entity traced by delicate
white bands that glowed with perfect regularity. The Ultimate White Banded Butterfly drifted past
the Unicorns, smoothing the turbulence left in the wake of its dark counterpart.
Black pursued White. White anticipated Black. Their chase formed a spiral. The spiral became
structure.

And into that structure burst a stripe of motion—quick, curious, impossible to contain.

The first Tiger-Cub...

The figure took its first step.

The Abyssalum became real.
