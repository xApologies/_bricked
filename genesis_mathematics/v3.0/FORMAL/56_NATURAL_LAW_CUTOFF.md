# 56 — NATURAL-LAW CUTOFF

This chapter is editorial and mathematical governance.

## Genesis Manuscript boundary

The Genesis Manuscript contains reusable mathematics.

It stops before any construction whose proof or meaning requires:

- a specific Time-Space realization;
- Time-Shell instantiation;
- a T0–T7 pipeline;
- an R0–R7 pipeline;
- a particular quantum field;
- QFT or QCD;
- gravity;
- any other domain-specific natural-law mechanism.

## Inclusion test

A result belongs in Genesis iff it can be stated and proved without requiring a particular physical realization.

Physics may generate new mathematics.

That mathematics may be abstracted into Genesis if its proof can be rewritten independently of the physical premise.

The physical application remains downstream.

## Editorial principle

\[
\boxed{
\text{Genesis contains the mathematics. Nature is an application of the mathematics.}
}
\]
