# 54 — AUTOMORPHISM GROUPS, RANK, AND \(SU(n)\) READINESS

Special unitary groups are mathematics and remain available to the Genesis Manuscript.

But the manuscript does not claim that a particular Genesis closure has already forced \(SU(2)\) or \(SU(3)\).

## Definition 54.1 — Genesis automorphism group

For closed persistent Genesis object \(P\),

\[
\operatorname{Aut}_G(P)
\]

is the total group of admitted identity-preserving automorphisms once such a total group is well-defined.

## Rank question

A group identification requires an independently established rank/dimension theorem.

Matrix size, number of visible rainbow basins, sphere label, or History length does not by itself establish rank.

## \(SU(n)\) readiness protocol

To earn an identification with \(SU(n)\), establish:

1. a complex \(n\)-dimensional realization;
2. a positive Hermitian invariant;
3. preservation of that invariant;
4. determinant-one/orientation normalization;
5. generation/completeness of all admitted transformations.

Only then may one conclude

\[
\operatorname{Aut}_G(P)\cong SU(n).
\]

## Current status

\(SU(2)\) and \(SU(3)\) remain available mathematical targets, not established Genesis closures.
