# 44 — HISTORY, PROVENANCE, AND IDENTITY

## Definition 44.1 — Witnessed identity

\[
X^w=(X,H_X,W_X,I_X),
\]

where \(H_X\) is ordered History, \(W_X\) is a recovery/provenance witness, and \(I_X\) is the identity contract.

## Principle 44.2 — Same readout is not same History

Two distinct ordered histories may terminate at the same current presentation.

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{
Q(H_1)=Q(H_2)
\not\Rightarrow
H_1=H_2
}
\]

for a general History quotient/readout \(Q\).

## Witness requirement

If a quotient or closure merges presentations, required provenance must survive in witness or be explicitly declared lost.

Silent History erasure is forbidden.
