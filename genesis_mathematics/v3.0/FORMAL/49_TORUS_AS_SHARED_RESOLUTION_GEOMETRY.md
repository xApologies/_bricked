# 49 — TORUS AS SHARED RESOLUTION GEOMETRY

The torus remains available in Genesis as pure geometry.

## Definition 49.1 — Cyclic Resolution coordinate

A cyclic Resolution phase may be represented by

\[
S^1.
\]

Returning to the same phase coordinate may retain different winding History.

## Definition 49.2 — Ordered pair torus

For two ordered cyclic Resolution coordinates,

\[
\boxed{
T^2=S^1_A\times S^1_B.
}
\]

With six rainbow sectors on each factor, the coarse ordered basin-pair partition contains

\[
6\times6=36
\]

cells.

## Principle 49.3 — Winding is not full History

Winding or net displacement is a quotient of ordered path History.

Different route words can have the same winding signature.

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{\text{winding}\neq\text{full ordered History}.}
\]

This chapter makes no claim that the torus is a fiber of any particular physical projection or natural-law domain.
