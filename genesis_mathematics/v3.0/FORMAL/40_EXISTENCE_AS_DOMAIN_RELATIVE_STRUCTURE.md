# 40 — EXISTENCE AS DOMAIN-RELATIVE STRUCTURE

## Definition 40.1 — Genesis domain

Let \(D\) be a bounded closed recursive/fractal domain admitted within the Genesis Field.

## Definition 40.2 — Existence state

For object/state \(x\), write

\[
\operatorname{Ex}_D(x)
\]

for the existence status of \(x\) relative to \(D\).

This is not forced to be a binary \(0/1\) variable.

Prime Zero retains its foundational meaning:

\[
0=\mathrm{DNE}.
\]

A realized existence state is defined by the domain's admitted boundary, occupancy, inheritance, persistence, and witness structure.

## Theorem 40.3 — Domain relativity

The statement that \(x\) exists is incomplete without scope.

The more precise Genesis question is:

\[
\boxed{\text{Does }x\text{ exist in }D\text{ under the admitted structure of }D?}
\]

Thus different domains may support different existence states for the same abstract candidate.

## Firewall

Undefined is not identified with DNE.

If a domain has not been constructed or its chirality/existence structure has not been studied, the corresponding state is left undefined rather than assigned Prime Zero.
