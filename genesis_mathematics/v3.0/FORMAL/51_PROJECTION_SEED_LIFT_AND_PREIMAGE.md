# 51 — PROJECTION, SEED, LIFT, AND PREIMAGE

Let

\[
\Pi:X\rightsquigarrow Y
\]

be a declared projection profile.

For \(y\in Y\), define the compatible preimage family

\[
\Pi^{-1}(y).
\]

A projected state is a seed/readout, not automatically the whole identity.

At Resolution \(R\), with Bandwidth and History constraints,

\[
\operatorname{AdmLift}_{R}(y)
\subseteq
\Pi^{-1}(y).
\]

Recursive refinement can narrow the admissible lift family.

The full projection/lift ancestry may therefore preserve information not present in the visible shadow alone.
