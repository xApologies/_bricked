# 47 — RAINBOW ROAD AS INFORMATION TRANSPORT

Rainbow Road is Genesis-native.

Its primitive job is information transport through admissible relational structure.

## Definition 47.1 — Domain-restricted path algebra

For Genesis domain \(D\) and Bandwidth \(B\),

\[
\mathsf{RR}_{D,B}
=
\operatorname{Path}\bigl(\operatorname{Gen}_D(B)\bigr).
\]

A route is an ordered composable word

\[
\gamma=a_n\circ\cdots\circ a_1.
\]

History records the actual word:

\[
H(\gamma)=(a_1,\dots,a_n).
\]

## Payload-independence principle

Rainbow Road constrains admissible transport independently of the downstream semantics assigned to the transported state.

Thus the same transport mathematics may later carry physical, computational, or other domain-specific interpretations without changing its Genesis role.

\[
\boxed{
\text{Rainbow Road}=\text{History-bearing information transport}.
}
\]
