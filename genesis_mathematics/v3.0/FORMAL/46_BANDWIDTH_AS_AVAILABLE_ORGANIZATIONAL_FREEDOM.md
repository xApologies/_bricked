# 46 — BANDWIDTH AS AVAILABLE ORGANIZATIONAL FREEDOM

## Definition

Bandwidth is:

\[
\boxed{\text{Available Organizational Freedom}.}
\]

Let

\[
\operatorname{Gen}_B(x)
\]

be the primitive transformations that are both structurally admissible and currently available at state \(x\).

Bandwidth may remove or restrict available transformations.

It does not invent transformations that are not part of the domain grammar.

It does not silently rewrite History.

## Recovery under Bandwidth

Recovery from \(y\) to the identity class of \(x\) is directed reachability in the Bandwidth-restricted History graph.

## Firewall

\[
\boxed{\text{Bandwidth is a filter, not a hidden preference rule.}}
\]
