# 52 — CONNECTION AND LIFT SELECTION

The Genesis selection architecture is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{History-decorated candidate lifts}
\to
\text{Bandwidth filter}
\to
\text{connection selection}
\to
\text{witness/completion gate}.
}
\]

Bandwidth restricts the family of currently available lifts.

It does not choose among all surviving lifts.

A connection supplies a rule for selecting an operative lift from the admissible family.

A witness/completion gate then determines whether the selected event is admitted into completed History.

This factorization is mathematical and does not require a physical field realization.
