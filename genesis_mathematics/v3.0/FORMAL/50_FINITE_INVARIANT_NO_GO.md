# 50 — FINITE INVARIANTS CANNOT ENCODE UNBOUNDED ORDERED HISTORY

## Theorem 50.1

Let \(H\) be an unbounded family of distinguishable ordered histories and let

\[
q:H\to F
\]

map into a finite set \(F\).

Then \(q\) cannot be injective on all of \(H\).

### Proof

By the pigeonhole principle, infinitely or sufficiently many distinct histories must share the same finite image. ∎

## Corollary 50.2

A finite holonomy, finite symmetry element, finite residue, or other finite-valued invariant cannot by itself faithfully encode an unbounded ordered History family.

This is the abstract Genesis theorem distilled from the chirality audits.

No particular physical holonomy is required for the proof.
