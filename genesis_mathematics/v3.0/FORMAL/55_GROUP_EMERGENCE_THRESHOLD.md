# 55 — GROUP-EMERGENCE THRESHOLD

The mathematics distilled from the chirality work does not require the original natural-law stage labels.

## Theorem 55.1 — Closure-before-total-group principle

It is lawful to ask for a total automorphism group of one identity only after the object has achieved a sufficiently closed persistent identity class.

Before persistent closure, one may study local transformations, semigroups, groupoids, path categories, or partial symmetries, but not silently assume one total symmetry group acts on a completed identity.

This is a pure Genesis theorem.

A later physical realization may instantiate this threshold at a particular stage, but the stage label is not part of the theorem.
