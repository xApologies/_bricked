# 53 — CHIRALITY AS ORDERED GEOMETRY

Chirality remains a Genesis term.

The manuscript uses chirality more generally than a simple left/right physical label.

## Definition 53.1

For a domain \(D\), chirality is the domain-relative oriented identity structure induced by ordered boundary, inheritance, recursive address, and admissible reversal.

Write schematically:

\[
\chi_D(x).
\]

## Chirality distinctions

Genesis chirality may distinguish:

- left/right or ordered orientation;
- orientation History;
- reversal behavior;
- complementary realization;
- persistence of orientation through projection;
- identity under admissible deformation.

## Cradle relation

The chirality seed is already present in the ordered Cradle.

Thus chirality is not imported into Genesis from physics.

A physical theory may later instantiate or interpret Genesis chirality, but that use is downstream.
