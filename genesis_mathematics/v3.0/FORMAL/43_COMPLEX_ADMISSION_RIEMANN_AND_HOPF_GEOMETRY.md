# 43 — COMPLEX ADMISSION, RIEMANN COMPACTIFICATION, AND HOPF-TYPE GEOMETRY

## Complex admission

The square-root event

\[
\sqrt{-1}=i
\]

admits a complex continuation rather than treating negative real address as a terminal obstruction.

## Riemann compactification

A complex plane may be compactified by adjoining the point at infinity, yielding the Riemann sphere as a mathematical carrier.

## Hopf-type geometry

Hopf-compatible maps and higher-dimensional compact carriers remain available as pure geometry.

They are not, by themselves, proofs that the Genesis structure has selected a particular physical gauge group.

## Construction-ancestry rule

A downstream compact representation may not erase the construction history that made its coordinates available.

Thus the manuscript retains:

\[
\boxed{\text{representation}\neq\text{construction ancestry}.}
\]
