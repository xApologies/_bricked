## Assumption A1 — Universal energy

For the present Genesis Manuscript, admit:

\[
\boxed{\mathbf A_1:\ \text{Energy is universal.}}
\]

This is an assumption, not a proved theorem.

The manuscript does not require energy to have one universal physical realization.

The canonical rainbow object is

\[
\boxed{\text{🌀}}
\]

The visible basin ordering is

\[
R\to O\to Y\to G\to B\to V.
\]

These are structural basins / energy-accessibility addresses.

## Definition 41.2 — Basin address

A basin address is a coarse structural coordinate in the rainbow organization.

The six visible basin addresses need not exhaust the underlying continuous carrier.

## Principle 41.3 — Structural before physical

The rainbow basins are part of Genesis as geometry of accessibility and organization.

A later physical theory may interpret or instantiate these basins as particular energy states, but that application is downstream of the mathematical structure.
