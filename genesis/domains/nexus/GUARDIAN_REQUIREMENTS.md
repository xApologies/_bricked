# Guardian requirements at Nexus

Status: WORKING requirements; Guardian mathematics OPEN.

Require the sequence well-formedness, mathematical validity, present admissibility, actor authorization, realization and closure. Admission or realization failure must remain distinguishable and witnessed.

Mathematical validity precedes present admissibility and actor authority. Authority selects admissible states; it cannot enlarge the mathematical state space. Require identity, reason, authority, route, realization, before/after state, History and provenance witnesses. This document specifies domain constraints, not Guardian cognition.
