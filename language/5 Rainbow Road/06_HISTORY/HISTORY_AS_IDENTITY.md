# History as Identity

A Road-run final Geometric inherits:

* every transformation/instantiation event already in the source;
* every Portal closure event;
* one `RAINBOW_ROAD_CLOSED` or failure-frontier event;
* every Portal receipt;
* the Road closure receipt.

The Road receipt stores the ordered intermediate instance IDs. A final object can therefore answer not only **where am I?** but **how did I get here?**
