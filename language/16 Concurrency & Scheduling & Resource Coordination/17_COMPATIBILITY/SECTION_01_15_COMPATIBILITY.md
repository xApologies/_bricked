# Compatibility with Sections 01–15

Section 16 is additive. It does not redefine:

- Section 01 fabric/storage hardware ABI.
- Section 02 Geometric instantiation.
- Section 03 immutable transformation/forking.
- Section 04 Portal transaction closure.
- Section 05 Rainbow Road composition.
- Section 06 QFT/GR typed transduction.
- Section 07 quantum information ownership/effects.
- Section 08 GIR/GVM semantic machine.
- Section 09 static type/effect linker.
- Sections 10–14 frontend, optimization, package, callable, and data/control semantics.
- Section 15 recursive frame and persistent-continuation semantics.

Section 16 schedules already-admitted work. A backend may exploit native CPU/GPU/FPGA parallelism only if it preserves the required dependency, resource, closure, ownership, provenance, and deterministic-observation contracts.
