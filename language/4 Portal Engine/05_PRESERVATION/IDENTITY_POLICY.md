# Portal Identity Policy

Section 04 permits only `PRESERVE_MMO` for successful full Geometric transportation.

The destination:

* has a new `instance_id`;
* retains `canonical_mmo_id`;
* retains `representation_id`;
* records `source_instance_id` as its parent/transport ancestor;
* inherits source history and appends `PORTAL_CLOSED`;
* inherits source provenance and appends the Portal closure receipt.

Identity derivation and representation-changing transduction belong to explicit later contracts and must not be smuggled into ordinary transport.
