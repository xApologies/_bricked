# Source crosswalk

Section 15 is an implementation synthesis over already-established Genesis/BLACKGLASS concepts:

- **Identity Through History** motivates inheritance of execution history as part of recoverable identity.
- **Holonomy & Chirality** motivates path-sensitive transport/history rather than treating traversal as unordered metadata.
- **Rainbow Road / Corridor** supplies the larger execution context in which recursive route discovery and recursive relationship traversal can occur.
- **Section 13** explicitly deferred recursion rather than pretending recursive call expansion was safe.
- **Section 14** explicitly deferred unbounded repetition so recursion and termination/closure could be designed together.

The exact recursion syntax, frame ISA, affine decrease checker, topology executor, and continuation format are Section 15 engineering constructions. They are not retroactively asserted to be wording from the earlier mathematical sources.
