# Corridor, Portal and Rainbow Road

Status: WORKING CANON; exact mathematical realization remains OPEN.

**Corridor** = mathematically admissible route/continuation; admissible possibility.

**Portal** = typed realized transfer/traversal over an admitted Corridor.

The single [Rainbow Road specification](../genesis/RAINBOW_ROAD.md) defines the persistent ordered composition/history of closed Portals. History participates in identity. A route's admissibility is distinct from its realized traversal; these definitions do not invent route-admission, color or propagation mathematics.

The [four-object information path](LAYER_ZERO_INFORMATION_PATH.md) remains `Sea <-> Shell <-> H6 <-> R5`; no direct bypass is introduced. [BRANE realization](BRANE_ROLE.md) is not the Geometric itself.
