# Geometric

Status: WORKING CANON; primitive and mathematical reconciliation remain OPEN.

A Geometric is a bounded, identity-bearing organization of Genesis-Field
occupancy whose internal state carries Chirality organization, whose relationships
become distinguishable under appropriate Resolution, and whose identity may be
inherited through admissible transformation.

It is NOT identical to its visualization, color readout, S2 projection, BRANE
realization, or host serialization.

Structural categories: boundary; occupancy; Chirality organization; relationships;
identity/inheritance; history; provenance.

A Geometric carries an authoritative Chirality configuration composed from one or
more Chirality structures/cells. Chirality Byte is the current candidate primitive
cell, not final canon.

Resolution and Bandwidth belong upstream to Genesis organization; they are not automatically stored Geometric properties. See the [unified framework](../architecture/UNIFIED_GENESIS_FRAMEWORK.md). The current [Byte](CHIRALITY_BYTE.md) and [Seed](CHIRALITY_SEED.md) organization does not resolve their OPEN mathematics.

## DP-006 architectural placement

Geometrics remain native bounded identity-bearing Genesis objects in a state-space substrate; the definition above is preserved. Conventional files may have State Geometric representations, but serialization is not object identity and its round-trip identity contract is OPEN. See [binary compatibility](../architecture/BINARY_COMPATIBILITY.md), [language crosswalk](GENESIS_LANGUAGE_ARCHITECTURE_CROSSWALK.md) and [DP-006](../../provenance/decisions/0007-dp006-cumulative-genesis-architecture.md).
