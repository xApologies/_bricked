# Guardian Intelligence Framework
Status: WORKING ARCHITECTURAL MODEL; canonical Guardian spec was OPEN at baseline.

Guardian is the top-level adaptive management/intelligence authority of the
instantiated Genesis Environment.

Guardian has system-wide potential write reach, but universal reach is not
mathematically unconstrained mutation.

Fundamental law:
Authority selects among mathematically admissible states; authority does not enlarge
the mathematical state space.

Guardian has maximum actor authority inside the Genesis domain and zero authority to
make a non-Genesis state valid by assertion.

Validation order:
1. well-formedness;
2. Genesis mathematical validity;
3. present admissibility;
4. actor authorization;
5. realization;
6. authoritative closure.

Guardian may occupy the highest actor-authorization class while remaining bounded by
steps 1-3 and by closure/invariants.

Guardian roles:
observe; maintain live system model; predict; recommend/select among admissible
possibilities; coordinate; verify; detect; respond; recover; manage.

Guardian should have universal management write authority but not invisible bypass
authority. Guardian-originated writes should traverse the appropriate Road/Nexus/
closure mechanisms and leave witnesses/receipts with identity, reason, authority,
route, realization, before/after state, History and provenance.

Under fibered Sea, investigate one Guardian identity/framework with distributed local
manifestations per computational fiber. Exact distribution/identity mathematics OPEN.

Guardian may itself have Genesis-native state and may self-update only through valid,
admissible Genesis transformations. It cannot authorize its own escape from Genesis
mathematical constraints.

Guardian is the active/intelligent member of the Hardware/Software/Guardian Trinity:
Hardware realizes; Software expresses; Guardian governs.

Distributed local Guardian manifestations are CANDIDATE. The existing [Guardian/CDN authority](GUARDIAN_CDN.md) stays OPEN for its full mathematical/security specification. Universal potential reach does not grant ambient wildcard capability to ordinary software: Section 17 declaration/admission boundaries remain intact. Exact authorization/admission, distributed identity and self-state mathematics remain OPEN.
